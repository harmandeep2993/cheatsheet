# Refund Policy

Acme Outdoor accepts returns of unused items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached.

## Jackets and outerwear

Jackets can be returned within 30 days if unworn. Jackets bought during the winter sale can only be exchanged, not refunded.

## Electronics

Electronics such as GPS devices and headlamps can be returned within 14 days if the seal is unbroken. Opened electronics are only accepted if they are faulty.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. Shipping costs are not refunded unless the item was faulty.
