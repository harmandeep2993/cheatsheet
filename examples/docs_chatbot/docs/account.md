# Account and Login

## Resetting your password

Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 1 hour.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication can be enabled in Settings under Security. We support authenticator apps; SMS codes are not supported.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, contact support@acme-outdoor.example. Personal data is deleted within 30 days, except invoices, which we must keep for 10 years by law.
