# Shipping

Standard shipping within Germany takes 2 to 4 business days and is free for orders over 50 EUR. Orders below 50 EUR cost 4.95 EUR to ship.

## International shipping

We ship to all EU countries in 4 to 8 business days for a flat fee of 9.95 EUR. We currently do not ship outside the EU.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves the warehouse. Order status can also be checked in the account area under "My orders".
